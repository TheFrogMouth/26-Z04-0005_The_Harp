\chapter{Objectives}

The primary objective of the Harp pedal is to deliver a unique sound that blends the tonal qualities of a harmonica with the versatility of electric guitar effects. Specific goals include:
\begin{itemize}
\item Achieving a realistic harmonica sound through advanced signal processing.
\item Ensuring user-friendly operation with intuitive controls.
\item Maintaining high audio fidelity and low noise levels.
\end{itemize}
