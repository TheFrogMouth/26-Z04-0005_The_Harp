The Harp utilizes a combination of digital signal processing (DSP) and analog filtering techniques to achieve its sound. Key components include:
\begin{itemize}
\item **Analog Filters:** Low-pass and high-pass filters are employed to shape the frequency response, mimicking the tonal characteristics of a harmonica.
\item **DSP Algorithms:** Custom algorithms process the input signal to emulate the dynamics and timbre of harmonica playing, including pitch modulation and reverb effects.
\end{itemize}
